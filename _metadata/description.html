Vehicle Readout

Vehicle Readout redesigns the floating displays above vehicles with compact cargo cards and configurable live information: vehicle name, speed, load percentage, operating state, next stop or destination depot, line name, condition, passenger happiness, and remaining cargo delivery time. Includes visibility filters, optional inclusion of followed vehicles and vehicles with open detail windows, and configurable update intervals.

Show speed above or below the floating readout, in the game bar, or both. The game bar can also show destination, line, operating state, total load, condition, or vehicle name for the followed or selected vehicle, with a full vehicle summary on hover.
